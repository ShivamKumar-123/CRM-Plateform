## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How long does it take to migrate from our existing system?**
Typical migration takes **5–7 business days**. Our team handles data cleanup, field mapping, and import. We support direct migration from Excel, Google Sheets, Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, and most other CRMs.

**Q: What if our team isn't tech-savvy?**
The platform is designed for non-technical users. We provide **live training sessions**, video tutorials, and an in-app help center. Most teams are fully productive within 2 days. Your sales team will thank you.

**Q: Can we self-host on our own servers?**
Yes — available on the **Enterprise plan**. We provide deployment guides for AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, and on-premise Linux servers. Your IT team retains full data control.

**Q: What happens to our data if we leave?**
You can **export everything** anytime as CSV/Excel from the admin panel. We provide a full database dump on request. After cancellation, your data is retained for 90 days for recovery, then permanently deleted.

**Q: Does it integrate with our existing tools?**
Yes — we offer integrations with WhatsApp Business, Razorpay, Google Drive, Dropbox, Slack, and any tool via webhooks. Custom integrations available on Enterprise plan.

**Q: How does multi-branch work?**
Each branch operates as a separate **organization** within the platform, with its own users, pipelines, and data. Owners can see consolidated reports across all branches. Perfect for franchises, multi-city offices, or holding companies.

**Q: What about GST invoicing?**
Built-in. Generate GST-compliant invoices with your company GSTIN, customer GSTIN, HSN/SAC codes. Tax computed automatically. Downloadable as PDF.

**Q: Can our customers see anything?**
Only what you choose to share. Each organization gets a **customer portal** where your customers can log support tickets, see their invoices, download their documents, and track their orders. They never see your team's internal data.

**Q: How does pricing scale?**
Per-user pricing. You only pay for the people actively using the system. Inactive users can be deactivated without losing their data — reactivate anytime.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
Yes — **14-day free trial** with full Pro features. No credit card required. Cancel anytime.
